Illness and symptoms

What to come to us with

Your GP is the first person to turn to when something is wrong. We examine you, start treatment and, when needed, send you to the right specialist.

  • Temperature and fever
  • Cough
  • Runny nose, rhinitis
  • Sore throat, tonsillitis
  • Sinusitis
  • Earache
  • Flu and colds
  • Abdominal pain
  • Diarrhoea and vomiting
  • Urinary infection, cystitis
  • Back and lower back pain
  • Migraine and headache
  • High blood pressure, hypertension
  • Infections and inflammation
  • Allergies

In a sudden serious emergency call the ambulance on 155.

For foreigners

Foreigners — all public insurers and PVZP

Any insurance

If you work in the Czech Republic, have permanent residence, temporary protection or study here, you are in the public system — most often VZP, but also OZP, ČPZP and others — and visits to your GP are covered by the insurer. We accept all public insurers (VZP, VoZP, ČPZP, OZP, ZPŠ, ZP MV, RBP) and PVZP comprehensive insurance for long-term residence. Different insurer? Call us and we will check.

FAQ

What you ask us

How much is a work medical and a driving licence certificate?

Pre-employment and exit medical 500 CZK, driving fitness certificate (also a change or extension) 500 CZK, one-off visit for an uninsured patient 1,000 CZK. Book online, Mon–Fri 10:00–12:00.

Do you really have free places?

Yes, MUDr. Éva Bojčuk is accepting new registrations. Call +420 606 755 784 and we will arrange everything.

Can I register if I live outside Ústí?

Yes. You are free to choose your GP regardless of where you live. We register patients from the whole Ústí Region.

How do I switch from my current doctor?

Simply register with us. You can change GP once every three months, and we request your records from your previous doctor ourselves.

Where can I find a GP in Ústí nad Labem?

Here: MUDr. Éva Bojčuk at Poliklinika U Vlečky accepts new patients from Ústí nad Labem, Děčín, Teplice and the whole Ústí Region. Call +420 606 755 784.

Do you issue certificates for driving school, work or school?

Yes. For registered patients we issue driving licence medicals, notes for employers, schools and insurers, food handler’s cards, sick notes and extracts from medical records. Some certificates are charged according to the practice’s price list.

Which insurers do you accept?

All Czech public health insurers — VZP, VoZP, ČPZP, OZP, ZPŠ, ZP MV, RBP — and PVZP commercial insurance for foreigners. Visits are paid by your insurer; you only bring your insurance card.

I have temporary protection (Ukraine) — can I register?

Yes. With temporary protection you are covered by Czech public health insurance (usually VZP), so we register you like any other patient. Bring your insurance card and your visa or ID.

Does the doctor speak English or Russian?

Yes — we speak English, Russian and Ukrainian as well as Czech. The diagnosis, treatment and paperwork are explained in a language you understand, no interpreter needed.

I have just moved to Ústí — how do I start?

Call +420 606 755 784. We confirm a free place and arrange your first visit. Bring your passport or residence card and insurance card; records from your previous doctor if you have them. Without a GP you cannot get sick leave, referrals or regular prescriptions, so it is worth registering early.

Do you accept foreigners with commercial insurance?

Yes, including PVZP comprehensive health insurance for foreigners. If you have a different commercial insurer, call us and we will check whether we can register you.
